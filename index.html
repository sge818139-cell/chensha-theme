Chapter 01 · Origin

The Red Stone of China 朱砂之源

Mined from the mountains of Guizhou and Hunan, cinnabar — zhūshā 朱砂 — has been ground, carved, and worn as a talisman since the Han Dynasty. It is the color of festivals, seals, and the protective red thread that wraps around every CHENSHA piece.

Chapter 02 · Craft

Meaning in Every Bead工 藝 與 意 涵

CHENSHA pieces are not simply accessories. Each stone, knot, and charm is chosen to carry a specific intention — so that what you wear every day also works for you.

I · 朱砂

The Stone

Genuine high-density cinnabar, hand-polished to a warm gloss. Believed in Chinese tradition to deflect negative energy and steady the spirit.

II · 編

Each cord is hand-woven with 72-linen thread using traditional Chinese knotting techniques — strong, comfortable, and made to last.

Pixiu, Hulu gourd, peace knot, five-petal flower — every charm is chosen for its centuries-old meaning, restyled for the modern wrist.
